About the Club

The CBIT Developer Student Club (DSC) is a student-led community where developers from CBIT come together to learn, collaborate, and build meaningful projects. We focus on improving coding skills, working on real-world problems, and connecting with tech communities.

What We Do

  • Organize coding bootcamps and tech talks
  • Host hackathons and ideathons
  • Collaborate on open-source and community projects
  • Prepare students for Google Developer Student Clubs and similar global initiatives

Get Involved

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced coder, there’s a place for you in DSC. Join us to network, learn new technologies, and make a real impact.
